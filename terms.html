Community Finder terms

Terms

Short, because the service is small.

Who can use it

You must be 18 or over to create an account. You confirm this at sign-up.

How to behave

Messaging here exists so people can arrange to do something together. Do not use it to harass, threaten, abuse, impersonate, solicit, scam, or send unwanted repeat contact. Do not use it to advertise. If someone declines or does not reply, that is the answer.

Accounts that do these things are suspended, which ends their sessions and removes them from discovery.

Blocking and reporting

You can block anyone, immediately and in both directions. Reporting someone also blocks them. Reports are read by whoever operates this server.

Meeting people

This software introduces people; it does not vet them. Accounts are not identity-verified, and a verified email proves only that an address works. Meet in public, tell someone where you are going, and leave if you want to. Use your judgement as you would with any stranger.

The listings

Organisations listed here are independent and have no relationship with this app. They are hand-checked and link-checked weekly, but groups fold, move and change their schedules. Confirm details on the organisation's own page before you turn up. Costs, timings and commitments described here may be out of date.

No warranty

This is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind. It may be unavailable, lose data, or be discontinued. Do not rely on it for anything that matters urgently.

Your content

What you write stays yours. You give whoever runs this server permission to store and display it for the purpose of making the app work — showing your messages to the person you sent them to, and your profile to people who share your visible interests.

Ending it

You can stop using it whenever you like and ask the operator to delete your account. The operator can suspend accounts that break these terms.
